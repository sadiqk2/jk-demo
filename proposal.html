2 · Technology & theme recommendation

Latest WordPress, with a theme that presents products like Sharpex

The reference store you shared (sharpex.com) gets its sharp, uncluttered product look from a hosted-commerce theme: white product stage, big crisp imagery, quick-view and clean cards. On WordPress — which is the better SEO choice and what you asked for — the same look is delivered by one of these WooCommerce themes:

ThemeLicence (approx.)Why it fits JKKVC
Flatsome ★ recommended$59 one-timeWorld's best-selling WooCommerce theme; sharp catalogue UX, quick-view, wishlist, excellent UX builder for your brand spec. Closest to the Sharpex look.
Blocksy (Pro)free / $89 yrFastest Core Web Vitals of the three; ideal if page-speed SEO is the top priority.
Astra (Pro)free / $59 yrLargest install base, huge starter-template library, very beginner-friendly for your team.
SEO note: no theme “is SEO” by itself — rankings come from structure, speed and content. Whichever theme we pick, we pair it with Rank Math (schema, sitemaps), WP Rocket + Cloudflare (speed), WebP/AVIF imagery and HPOS checkout. WordPress remains the strongest SEO platform of the three major options (WordPress > Shopify > Wix in independent comparisons).

Reference content-management resources your team can browse: wordpress.org (CMS), woocommerce.com (store engine), flatsome.io / blocksy.io / astra.io (theme demos), rankmath.com (SEO plugin), razorpay.com (payments). The mock website you are viewing demonstrates the target look on exactly this stack's design language.

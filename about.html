Our story

From a fertilizer counter to Kashmir's agri backbone

Founded in 2010 by Farooq Jan Para with a focus on fertilizers, JK Krishi Vikas Cooperative Ltd added seeds and gardening tools in 2013 and stepped into modern farm machinery in 2015. Today JKKVC is a premier wholesaler and authorised distributor for national and multinational brands — supplying lawn care equipment, agriculture machinery, horticulture tools, insecticides, pesticides, seeds and fertilizers across the Valley.

We take pride in delivering products of impeccable quality at competitive prices, with timely order fulfilment and a scientific, progressive approach to customer service — so that every farmer we serve grows more, with less effort.
